# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Stack
Delegated by user: Three.js, TypeScript and Vite. Build directly in code.
## Users
The drone builder inspecting a compact PCB and its mechanical assembly before routing.
## Product Purpose
Inspect the actual placement together with motor, propeller, battery and attachment envelopes.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Orbit, exploded view, visibility toggles and component inspection. Separate pcb/ and cad/ folders. Vercel hosting. PCB is two layers, top-side assembly, 80 mm motor diagonal and 46 mm props. Routing excluded. Mechanical envelopes are provisional.
## Evidence on Hand
Two supplied design specifications; empty initial repository. No measured thrust, mass or mechanical-fit data.
